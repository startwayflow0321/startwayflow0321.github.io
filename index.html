VICE Uploader

VICE Uploader is a private publishing tool used by the creator of the VICE channel (GTA 6 news and secrets in Spanish) to publish the channel's own original short videos to its own TikTok account (@vicegta6.es).

The tool runs on the creator's computer. It uses TikTok's official Content Posting API to upload each finished video with its caption, so the creator can review and publish it. It is not offered to the public and does not access other users' accounts.
