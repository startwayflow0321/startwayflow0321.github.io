Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What we access

VICE Uploader is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @vicegta6.es. When the owner authorizes the app with TikTok Login Kit, it receives an access token and basic profile information (open id, display name, avatar) of that account, solely to upload the owner's own videos.

How data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish videos created by the owner to the owner's TikTok account and to show the upload status. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never published. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

Other users

The app does not collect data from viewers or from any account other than the owner's.

Contact

Questions: GitHub Issues.
